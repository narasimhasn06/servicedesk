# Positioning

## Formula

I help **[who]** **[get what result]** by **[how]**, without **[pain]**.

## Headline options

1. **Result-first:** I help small businesses stop copy-pasting: forms, CRM, database and AI connected with n8n and .NET.
2. **Niche-first:** Automation & API developer for coaches, clinics and agencies — leads, bookings and payments that run themselves.
3. **Trust-first:** I build automations and AI features with clear limits — what data AI sees, what it outputs, what happens when it fails.

**Chosen:** I help small businesses stop copy-pasting: forms, CRM, database and AI connected with n8n and .NET.

## One-line offer

I help small businesses remove manual work: I connect your tools with n8n, build the APIs behind them in .NET,
and add AI only where it saves real time — with clear limits on what data it sees.

## The four services

| Service | Pitch |
|---|---|
| Workflow automation | I connect your forms, email, CRM, spreadsheets and databases with n8n so leads, bookings and reports move automatically — with error alerts so nothing fails silently. |
| .NET development | I build and extend ASP.NET Core Web APIs and internal business apps — clean structure, documented endpoints, authentication, and deployment you can hand to any developer. |
| AI integration | I add one focused AI feature to your existing app — with clear limits on what data the AI can see, a fixed output format, and a fallback when it fails. |
| Database & API work | I fix broken integrations, speed up slow SQL queries, and connect your app to third-party services like payment gateways, CRMs and messaging APIs. |

## Words to avoid

passionate · synergy · cutting-edge · guru · ninja · "10+ years" (unless true)
